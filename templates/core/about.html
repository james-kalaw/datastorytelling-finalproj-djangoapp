About the project

A multidimensional look at underemployment and job quality in the Philippines

The Shape of Work is a data story built for DS 009 – Data Storytelling at the Technological Institute of the Philippines – Quezon City. It asks what the headline unemployment rate leaves out.

The Three Questions

  1. How Has Underemployment Compared With Unemployment in the Philippines From 2005 to 2026? Trends, 2005–2026.
  2. How Does Underemployment Vary Across Provinces, Sex, Age Groups and Sectors? Impact.
  3. How Are Working Hours, Education and Pay Related to Underemployment? Reality.

How We Built It

PSA Labor Force Survey tables were cleaned and reshaped in Power Query, modeled in Power BI as a galaxy schema with DAX measures, and published to the Power BI Service. Inside the report, the province map is a native Azure Maps visual so it displays in the web embed. The model tables were exported with DAX Studio and cleaned with Python into eight datasets. This Django site embeds each dashboard page, draws the Python visuals on the server (a GeoPandas province map, a matplotlib education chart and one results chart per sub-question), and serves the datasets.

Data Sources

Philippine Statistics Authority: OpenSTAT Labor Force Survey tables (2005–2026), Provincial Labor Market Statistics (2023–2025), and the June 2026 LFS press-release Tables 3 and C.

Limits of This Study

Findings come from published aggregate tables, so they describe patterns, not causes. Coverage windows differ by table, and 2025–2026 figures are partly preliminary.
