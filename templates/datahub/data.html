How to search
Type a name or topic. A province or city (Sarangani, Makati), a sector (Agriculture), an industry (Manufacturing), an education level (College Graduate), an age group or sex (Female, 15-24), or a reason (holidays).
Questions work too. Words like highest, top or worst sort from the highest value; lowest or best sort from the lowest. "Which province has the highest rate?" lists provinces from the top.
Everyday words are understood. Women, men, youth, elderly, farm, factory, salary, school, why, and the island groups Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao.
What it can't do. This is a search over our PSA data, not an AI. It can't explain causes, forecast, or answer questions outside the {{ total_items }} values listed here. Use the buttons below to filter by type.
